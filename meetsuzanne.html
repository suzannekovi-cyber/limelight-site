Meet Suzanne

Dedicated to helping actors reach their goals.

Suzanne Kovi is a SAG-AFTRA actress, speaker, coach and creator with more than three decades of experience in television, film, commercials, public speaking and storytelling.

Work With Suzanne
Actor & Coach

She knows both sides of the audition.

Known for helping actors uncover authentic performances, Suzanne combines technical expertise with coaching insight to create auditions that feel natural, compelling and memorable.

As a working actor herself, and winner of Best Supporting Actress for the short film There's Something I Have to Tell You, she knows what it takes to walk into the room ready.

Her goal is simple: help every actor walk away confident that they submitted their very best work.

My Motto

You are more than you think. Do not doubt your ability to play big, bring value and know your worth. If a role challenges you, take it. It's not about booking, it's about showing up ready.

Not sure if you have what it takes?

Try, or you will never know.
